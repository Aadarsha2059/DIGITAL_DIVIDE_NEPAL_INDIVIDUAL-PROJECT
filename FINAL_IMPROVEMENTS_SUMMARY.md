### **1. ✨ Enhanced Prescriptive Recommendations**

#### **Previous Issues:**
- Generic recommendations
- Not specific to district conditions
- Limited analysis depth

#### **New Features:**
✅ **Comprehensive Analysis** - 10 priority levels of recommendations
✅ **District-Specific** - Tailored to each district's unique situation
✅ **Data-Driven** - Based on all metrics and historical trends
✅ **Actionable** - Specific steps, timelines, and budget estimates
✅ **Year-Aware** - Considers historical growth rates

#### **New Recommendation Categories:**

1. **Critical Infrastructure Gaps**
   - Electricity access analysis with specific percentages
   - Cost estimates for infrastructure development
   - Partnership recommendations (Nepal Electricity Authority)

2. **Internet Connectivity Levels**
   - Critical (<15%): Immediate 4G tower deployment, fiber backbone
   - Urgent (<30%): Public-private partnerships, last-mile solutions
   - Moderate (<50%): 5G upgrades, affordable packages
   - Specific budget allocations per population

3. **Urban-Rural Digital Divide**
   - Gap analysis with exact percentages
   - Targeted rural connectivity programs
   - Community digital center recommendations

4. **Digital Literacy Integration**
   - Literacy rate thresholds with specific actions
   - School computer lab programs
   - Adult literacy initiatives

5. **Telecommunications Infrastructure**
   - Mobile network expansion strategies
   - Affordable smartphone programs
   - Coverage gap analysis

6. **Strategic Opportunities**
   - Leverage existing TV/Radio infrastructure
   - Cable network partnerships for internet
   - Community engagement through radio

7. **Growth Trajectory Analysis**
   - Historical growth rate calculations
   - Future projections with timelines
   - Stagnation warnings and interventions

8. **Population-Based Strategies**
   - Large population (>400K): Scalable solutions, multiple ISPs
   - Small population (<200K): Cost-effective wireless solutions

9. **Integrated Development**
   - Electricity-internet synergy analysis
   - Smart grid integration opportunities
   - Digital payment infrastructure

10. **Implementation Timelines**
    - Year-by-year action plans
    - Specific milestones and targets
    - Success indicators

#### **Example Enhanced Recommendation:**
